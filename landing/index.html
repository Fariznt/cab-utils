How it works

Text the number and it asks a couple of quick questions: which course you're waiting on, and which section. Confirm your pick and you're set. It takes about a minute, and you don't need to know any special commands or formats.

Then it goes quiet. When a seat opens in a section you're watching, you get a text telling you which course and section it was, so you can go register.

— a few days later —
A seat just opened in CSCI 0320 S01. Register now at cab.brown.edu.

Example messages. The exact wording changes as the service gets better.

Alerts are one-shot. Once you've been texted about a section, that alert is finished, so you won't keep getting pinged about a course you already grabbed. You can watch more than one section at a time, and you can cancel any of them whenever you want.

Texting commands

Two words always work, from anywhere in the conversation, whatever else is going on.

Text thisWhat happens
HELP Shows you what you can do (set up an alert, see the ones you already have, cancel one) along with contact info, from wherever you are in the conversation.
STOP Cancels every alert you have and stops all messages from Seat Signal. You'll get one confirmation text and then nothing else, ever. You can always start again later by texting the number.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Seat Signal is free. Your carrier's normal message and data rates apply, the same as any other text you send.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Your phone number is your account. There's no password to forget and no website to log into. Texting the number is what signs you up, and it's the only thing that does.

Can someone else sign up my number as a prank?

No. Alerts only ever go to the number that texted in. There's no form anywhere that lets one person enter another person's number, and numbers are never imported, purchased, or added from any other source. If you're getting messages, it's because that phone sent the first text.

How many texts will I get?

It depends entirely on how many alerts you set up. Setting one up takes a handful of messages back and forth, and then it's quiet until a seat actually opens, which is one more text. If no seat opens, you hear nothing.

How do I make it stop?

Reply STOP to any message and everything stops immediately. If you'd rather drop just one alert and keep the rest, text the number and it'll walk you through it.

What do you do with my phone number?

It's stored encrypted, and it's used for exactly two things: knowing who you are when you text in, and sending you your alerts. It's shared only with the licensed messaging provider that actually delivers the texts, because there's no way to send you a message otherwise.

It is never sold, rented, or given to anyone else. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. There is no marketing here at all, and there never will be. Text STOP to stop messages, or email the address below and your number and everything attached to it will be deleted.

What user information do you keep?

  • Your phone number, encrypted.
  • Your messages with the service, so the conversation can pick up where it left off.

Is this official? Does Brown run it?

No. Seat Signal is an independent tool built by a student. It reads the same public course listings you can see yourself and checks them so you don't have to.

Can I rely on it to get into a class?

Please don't rely on it as your only plan. Seat Signal is provided free and as-is, with no guarantee that an alert will arrive, arrive on time, or be correct. Course data comes from public listings and can be delayed or wrong. An alert tells you a seat appeared, it doesn't hold one for you, and someone else may take it first. Always confirm in your school's official registration system.
